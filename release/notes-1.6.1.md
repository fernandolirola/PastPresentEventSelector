**Past & Present Event Selector (PPES) 1.6.1**

**Spawn editor**
Zones can now be removed one at a time without replacing the rest of the list. Clearing the field removes that zone override and restores the map's original zones.

**Reserve zone label**
The raid preview displays SPT's `ZoneRailStrorage` key as "Rail Storage" while keeping the original key for compatibility.

**Requirements:** MoreBotsAPI 2.1.1 or newer (server and client). Optional: Black Division (Blackout), Blackout by Vultify (Blackout darkness), SCOURGE (Scourge Incursion), WTT-ContentBackport (QBZ-191 reward).

**Install:** extract the zip into your game folder and overwrite. Every player needs the client files (prepatcher and plugin); with Fika, the host and headless client too.
